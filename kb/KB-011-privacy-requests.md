title: Requests to access, export or delete personal data
queues: [Customer Service, General Inquiry]
keywords: gdpr ccpa privacy personal data delete export erasure access request data subject retention
---
Requests to access, export or delete personal data are handled by the privacy team within 30 days as required by law, and support agents do not action them directly.

Steps:
1. Reply confirming the account email and whether you want an export, a deletion, or both.
2. The privacy team verifies identity and confirms the request within five business days.
3. Deletion is permanent and includes backups after the 35-day retention window.

Account data needed for invoicing is kept for the legally required period even after deletion.
