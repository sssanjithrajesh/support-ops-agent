id: KB-004
title: Reporting a suspected security incident or data breach
queues: [Technical Support, IT Support]
keywords: security breach unauthorized access hacked compromised phishing suspicious incident data exposure encryption vulnerability
---
Suspected security incidents are handled by the security team, not by front-line support, and they are acknowledged within one hour around the clock.

Steps:
1. Do not delete logs, reset passwords, or change settings until the security team has confirmed what to preserve.
2. Reply with the time window, the accounts involved, and what you observed. Screenshots help; pasted credentials do not, and we will redact them.
3. The security team will open an incident record and give you a single point of contact within one hour.

Support agents cannot confirm or deny a breach; only the incident report can.
