Planned maintenance is announced seven days ahead on the status page, and unplanned outages are posted there within ten minutes of detection with updates at least every 30 minutes.

Steps:
1. Check the status page for an active incident matching your symptoms; subscribe to get email or SMS updates.
2. If nothing is posted and several users are affected, reply with the time it started, the region, and an example request id. On-call is paged for confirmed multi-user impact.
3. After an incident, a summary with root cause and follow-up actions is published within five business days.

Single-user problems are usually configuration rather than an outage; see the troubleshooting article first.
