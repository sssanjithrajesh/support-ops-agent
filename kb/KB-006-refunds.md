Refunds are available in full within 14 days of a new purchase or renewal, and pro-rated after that for annual plans. A refund request is reviewed by a person, not granted automatically.

Steps:
1. Reply with the invoice number and the reason for the request.
2. A billing agent reviews it within two business days and confirms the amount before anything is processed.
3. Approved refunds return to the original payment method in five to ten business days.

If you are considering a chargeback, please contact us first; a chargeback closes the account while the dispute is open.
