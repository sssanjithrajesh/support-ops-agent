Most access problems are a stale password or a two-factor device change, and both can be fixed in under five minutes without waiting for an agent.

Steps:
1. Open the sign-in page and choose "Forgot password". The reset link is valid for 30 minutes.
2. If the account is locked after repeated attempts, the lock clears automatically after 15 minutes.
3. If you changed phones, use one of the backup codes issued when two-factor was enabled. If you have none, reply to this ticket from the email address on the account and an agent will verify identity by phone before resetting two-factor.

We never ask for a password over email or chat.
