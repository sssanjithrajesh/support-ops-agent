Hardware can be returned within 30 days for any reason, and defective units are replaced under warranty for one year. Both start with a return authorisation number.

Steps:
1. Reply with the order number and, for a defect, a short description of what fails.
2. We issue a return authorisation and a prepaid shipping label within one business day.
3. Refunds or replacements are processed within three business days of the unit arriving at the warehouse.

Please keep the original packaging where possible; damage in transit without it is not covered.
