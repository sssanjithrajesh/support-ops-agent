Plan features and list prices are published on the pricing page, and a sales engineer can walk through a live demo or a tailored quote within two business days.

Steps:
1. Compare plans on the pricing page; every plan includes a 14-day trial with no card required.
2. For a demo or a quote covering more than 50 seats, reply with your team size and the systems you need to integrate with.
3. A sales engineer will propose two or three time slots for a call.

Support agents do not negotiate pricing; the sales team does.
