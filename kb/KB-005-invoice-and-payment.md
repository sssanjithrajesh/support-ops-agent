Invoices are issued on the first of the month for the previous month's usage, and a declined payment is retried three times over ten days before service is affected.

Steps:
1. Download the invoice from Billing > Invoices to check the line items; usage-based lines show the meter and the unit price.
2. If a payment was declined, update the card under Billing > Payment method. The next retry runs within 24 hours.
3. If you believe you were charged twice, reply with both transaction ids; duplicates are reversed within five business days.

Billing agents can adjust an invoice but cannot change plan pricing; that is handled by your account contact.
