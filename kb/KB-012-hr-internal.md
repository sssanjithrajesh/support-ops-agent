HR and payroll questions from staff are routed to the people team and are not visible to customer-facing agents.

Steps:
1. Check the internal handbook first; leave balances and payslips are under Self-service.
2. For anything about your own pay or a colleague, reply here and the ticket moves to the confidential HR queue.
3. The people team answers within two business days.

This queue is internal; customer tickets misfiled here are moved back to Customer Service.
